# Fonts

The optional text fonts and writing styles of Korwus. `make fonts` cuts each one down to GB2312
and every character in the library (`qorwus/font.py`) and renames it, as the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 asks of a Modified Version. The licence is in `OFL.txt` beside this file.

Each is cut and renamed: `noto-serif.ttf` to Korwus Song, `wenkai.ttf` to Korwus Kai,
`fangsong.ttf` to Korwus Fangsong, `xingshu.ttf` to Korwus Xing and `caoshu.ttf` to Korwus Cao.
None of them is the original font, and none uses a Reserved Font Name.
